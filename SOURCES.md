# Research and media sources

This register supports the science facts and the NASA/JPL images referenced in the current MarsBound v16 candidate. The in-game survival model and event effects are simplified examples, not mission engineering specifications.

## Mars facts and environment

- [Mars at a Glance — NASA/JPL](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/press_kits/insight/launch/facts/mars-at-a-glance/) — Martian day length, orbital period, atmospheric composition and pressure, temperatures, moons, Olympus Mons, and other basic planetary facts.
- [Mars Fact Sheet — NASA/NSSDC](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/marsfact.html) — planetary size, gravity, number of moons, and average solar irradiance at Mars compared with Earth.
- [Mars 2020 Quick Facts — NASA/JPL](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/press_kits/mars_2020/landing/quick_facts/) — Mars size, gravity, year, day length, atmospheric density, and temperature.
- [Mars Communications Disruption and Delay — NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/mars-communications-disruption-and-delay.pdf) — communication delay and blackout considerations for Mars missions.
- [CHAPEA 2: Audio Log 3 — NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/podcasts/houston-we-have-a-podcast/chapea-2-audio-log-3/) — describes one-way Earth–Mars radio delay as variable with the planets' positions; its example discusses a delay of roughly 13–22 minutes.
- [Mars 2020 Quick Facts — NASA/JPL](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/press_kits/mars_2020/landing/quick_facts/) — baseline planetary facts, including Mars receiving less sunlight than Earth.

## Gale Crater, Curiosity, and surface resources

- [Curiosity Science Highlights — NASA Science](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/msl-curiosity/science-highlights/) — evidence for ancient rivers and lakes in Gale Crater, Yellowknife Bay, clay-bearing terrain, and Curiosity's exploration.
- [Curiosity Checks Under the Dust on a Martian Rock — NASA Photojournal, PIA22067](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/curiosity-checks-under-the-dust-on-a-martian-rock/) — Curiosity's brushed rock target near Vera Rubin Ridge; the purple tint suggested fine-grained hematite, an iron-oxide mineral.
- [Scoop Marks in the Sand at Rocknest — NASA Photojournal, PIA16469](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/scoop-marks-in-the-sand-at-rocknest) — Curiosity's soil sampling at Rocknest.
- [Windblown Sand from the Rocknest Drift — NASA Photojournal, PIA16570](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/windblown-sand-from-the-rocknest-drift) — the Rocknest sand drift.
- [Curiosity on the Clay Unit — NASA Photojournal, PIA23047](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/curiosity-on-the-clay-unit/) — Curiosity at the clay-bearing unit.
- [Curiosity Surveys the Clay-Bearing Unit — NASA Photojournal, PIA23139](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/curiosity-surveys-the-clay-bearing-unit/) — clay-bearing terrain surveyed by Curiosity.
- [View of Yellowknife Bay Formation with Drilling Sites — NASA/JPL, PIA17595](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/images/pia17595-view-of-yellowknife-bay-formation-with-drilling-sites/) — image of Yellowknife Bay and Curiosity drilling locations.
- [In-Situ Resource Utilization — NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/jsc-in-situ-resource-utilization/) — processing resources found on the Moon or Mars for future exploration; useful context for explaining that raw soil is not immediately usable metal or solar-cell material.
- [NASA Mars Rover Fully Analyzes First Soil Samples — NASA/JPL](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/nasa-mars-rover-fully-analyzes-first-soil-samples/) — mineralogical analysis of Curiosity's Rocknest samples.

## Mission examples used in the game

- [Ice on Mars — Now You See It — NASA Photojournal, PIA10910](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/ice-on-mars-now-you-see-it/) — subsurface ice exposed by the Phoenix Mars lander.
- [NASA's Oxygen-Generating Experiment MOXIE Completes Mars Mission — NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/mars-2020-perseverance/perseverance-rover/nasas-oxygen-generating-experiment-moxie-completes-mars-mission/) — MOXIE's oxygen production, final run, and purity results.
- [This Is One Mars Rover With MOXIE — NASA Photojournal, PIA23154](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/this-is-one-mars-rover-with-moxie/) — image of the MOXIE experiment being installed in the Perseverance rover.
- [Growing Plants in Space — NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/exploration-research-and-technology/growing-plants-in-space/) — NASA's Veggie plant-growth system and crops grown and eaten aboard the International Space Station.
- [Shades of Martian Darkness — NASA Photojournal, PIA22521](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/shades-of-martian-darkness/) — simulated views of a dust-darkened Martian sky from Opportunity's perspective.
- [Dusty Solar Panels on Spirit — NASA Photojournal, PIA10128](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/dusty-solar-panels-on-spirit/) — dust covering Spirit's solar panels and reducing electrical power.
- [A Great Place to Watch the Weather — NASA Photojournal, PIA04187](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/a-great-place-to-watch-the-weather-vertical/) — Spirit's image of dust devils; NASA describes wind events that cleared dust from the rover's solar panels.
